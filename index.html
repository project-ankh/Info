Meaning of Kore

The name Kore comes from ancient Greek and means “maiden” or “pure young one.” Historically linked with Persephone, Kore symbolises purity, renewal, nature, growth and transformation. In older traditions, the name was associated with springtime, cycles of rebirth and sacred symbolism connected to the natural world. Kore represents calmness, quiet strength, reflection and inner balance. This space is inspired by wellness, symbolism and peaceful guidance.
